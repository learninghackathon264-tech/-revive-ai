About this project (SIH pitch)

Problem

Indian homes throw away working-in-part electronics because people cannot tell what is wrong or what a fix costs. India is among the world’s largest e-waste producers.

Solution

REVIVE AI diagnoses a fault from a short description, says whether to fix, repair, reuse or recycle, shows repair cost against buying new, and tracks e-waste avoided.

How it works

Fault knowledge base for common household devices, AI diagnosis from text and photo, offline smart search, a reuse board, QR repair records, and Tamil, Hindi and English support.

Impact

Less landfill, lower household spending, and more work for local repair technicians.

Feasibility

A single web app with no special hardware. Next steps: verified technician directory, authorised recycler partnerships, and city-level pilots.

Costs are typical Indian market estimates, not quotes. Unplug a device before opening it, and leave mains-powered repairs to a technician if you are unsure.
